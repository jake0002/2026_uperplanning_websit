## 2. Research Services

| Method | What it is | Best for |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Focus group (FGI) | A moderated group discussion with 4–6 participants | Exploring needs, reactions, and perceptions |
| In-depth interview (IDI) | A one-on-one interview | Understanding motivations and decision-making in depth |
| Usability test (UT) | Participants complete real tasks on your app or website | Finding where and why users get stuck |
| Remote research | Interviews and UT by video call or with tools such as Maze | Participants in other regions or overseas |
| Prototype & MVP test | Tests with Figma prototypes, coded prototypes, or MVP apps used for 1–2 weeks | Validating a product before launch |

## 9. FAQ

**Q. Can you run user research in English?**

A. Yes, we can. Our moderator can run interviews and usability tests in English. For other languages, we arrange a live interpreter, and we can deliver the final report in English.

**Q. Can we hire you for participant recruiting only?**

A. Yes, you can. You can ask us to recruit participants only, handle incentive payments only, or run the entire study from design to report.

**Q. How long does participant recruiting take in Korea?**

A. It usually takes 5–10 days. Hard-to-reach groups such as professionals or very specific user conditions may take longer.

**Q. How much are participant incentives in Korea?**

A. For focus groups, incentives are usually KRW 100,000–200,000 per person. Professionals such as doctors, lawyers, and teachers usually require higher incentives. SUPERPLANNING pays each participant directly.

**Q. Can you recruit foreign residents in Korea?**

A. Yes, we can. We recruit foreign residents from China, Japan, Vietnam, Nepal, Uzbekistan, the U.S., and many other countries, based on nationality, length of stay, and Korean language level.

**Q. Do you run remote research?**

A. Yes, we do. We run remote interviews and remote usability tests by video call or with tools such as Maze. We also run remote tests with overseas users in the U.S., Japan, and other countries.

**Q. Where are in-person sessions held?**

A. In-person sessions are held at a dedicated UX research lab in Nonhyeon-dong, Gangnam-gu, Seoul. The lab rental fee is included in the quote.

**Q. How is a project priced?**

A. Pricing depends on the number of groups, participant conditions, and study scope. After a short consultation, we send a detailed quote right away.
