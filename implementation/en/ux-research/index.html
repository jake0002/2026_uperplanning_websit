1. Who We Help

We work with teams that need reliable user insights from Korea,
delivered in a format their global team can use right away.

  • Global brands entering Korea — validate your app, website, or store with real Korean users before or after launch.
  • Global HQ and product teams — get Korean user insights in English, without managing local vendors yourself.
  • Research agencies abroad — work with a local partner for recruiting, moderation, and facilities in Seoul.
  • Brands running global stores — compare your Korean store and overseas store (Shopify, Cafe24, etc.) country by country.

2. Research Services

We design each study around the decision you need to make,
then choose the right method for it.

6. Process & Timeline

  1. Kickoff — we define goals, target users, method, and schedule with you.
  2. Study design — we write the screener, discussion guide, and test tasks.
  3. Recruiting — we recruit and confirm participants (usually 5–10 days).
  4. Sessions — we run the sessions in person or remotely and record them.
  5. Analysis — we analyze behavior and comments, and rank issues by severity.
  6. Report — we deliver findings and UI/UX recommendations, in English if needed.

A typical project takes 2–3 weeks from design to final report.
Tight deadlines can be discussed.

7. Research Lab in Seoul

In-person sessions are held at a dedicated UX research lab in Nonhyeon-dong, Gangnam-gu, Seoul.
Your team can watch participants from the observation room monitor.
The lab rental fee is included in the quote.
